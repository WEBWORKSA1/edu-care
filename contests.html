  1. Pick a format: a written tip (100–400 words), a short essay, an infographic, or a video up to 2 minutes.

Official rules (summary)

No purchase necessary

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win. A purchase will not improve your chances of winning.

Eligibility

Open to individuals aged 13 or older. Entrants under 18 (or the age of majority where they live) must have permission from a parent or guardian, who may be required to accept the prize on their behalf. Employees of Edu.Care and their immediate families are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

Entry period

Each contest runs from the first day to the last day of the calendar month (11:59 p.m. Eastern Time). One entry per person per month.

Selection and notification

Winners are selected by judging, not at random, using the published criteria. Winners are notified by email and must respond within 10 days or an alternate winner may be chosen.

Prizes

Prize descriptions and values are announced on this page for each month. Prizes are non-transferable; no cash substitution except at the sponsor’s discretion. Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes.

Content license and conduct

Entries must be original, appropriate for all ages, and must not infringe anyone’s rights. By entering, you grant Edu.Care a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to publish your entry with credit. Entries with personal information about others, hateful content or plagiarism are disqualified.

Privacy

Entry information is used to run the contest as described in our privacy policy. This contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Google, YouTube or any social platform.
