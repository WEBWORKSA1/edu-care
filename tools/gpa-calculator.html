How this calculator works

Each grade is converted to points on a 4.0 scale (A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3 and so on), multiplied by its credits, summed and divided by total credits. The weighted GPA adds 0.5 points for honors and 1.0 point for AP/IB courses on passing grades, a common convention. Schools vary, so check your own school’s policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is a weighted or unweighted GPA more important?

Both appear on many transcripts. Colleges often recalculate GPA using their own method and look closely at course rigor, so a challenging schedule matters even when the unweighted GPA is slightly lower.

Do pass/fail courses count?

Usually not. Pass/fail, withdrawn and incomplete courses are typically excluded from GPA. Leave them out here.

How can I raise my GPA quickly?

Focus on the highest-credit course where you can move up a grade step, and use our final grade calculator to set a target for the exam.
