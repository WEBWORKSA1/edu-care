How to use focus blocks well

  1. Choose one specific task before you press start.
  2. Put your phone in another room and close unrelated tabs.
  3. When distracted, jot the thought on paper and return to the task.
  4. On breaks, stand up, move, drink water. Avoid scrolling.
  5. After four blocks, take a longer break and review what you did.
