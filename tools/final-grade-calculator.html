Final Grade Calculator

Find out exactly what you need on your final exam to reach your target grade.

You need on the final—

The formula

Required final score = (Target − (1 − Final weight) × Current grade) ÷ Final weight

Example: you have 82%, want 85%, and the final is worth 25%. (85 − 0.75 × 82) ÷ 0.25 = 94%. That tells you the exam needs a strong performance, so plan your revision with retrieval practice and timed past papers.
