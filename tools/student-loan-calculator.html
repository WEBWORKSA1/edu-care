How the estimate works

This uses the standard amortization formula for a fixed-rate loan with equal monthly payments. Real loans may differ: some have variable rates, fees, grace periods, interest that accrues while in school, or income-based repayment plans. Use the result as a planning estimate and confirm with your lender or official student aid office.

Ways to borrow less and repay faster

  • Exhaust grants and scholarships first; see Paying for college, explained.
  • Pay interest while in school if you can, so it does not capitalize.
  • Even small extra payments, applied to principal, cut total interest noticeably.
  • Keep total borrowing near or below your expected first-year salary.
