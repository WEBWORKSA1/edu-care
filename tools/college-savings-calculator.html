Tips for saving for education

  • Start early. Time does more work than contribution size, thanks to compounding.
  • Automate it. A fixed monthly transfer on payday is easier than deciding each month.
  • Use tax-advantaged accounts where available, such as 529 plans in the US, RESPs in Canada or similar schemes elsewhere. Check official government sources for current rules.
  • Ask family to contribute to education savings instead of toys for birthdays.
  • Plan for the gap. Savings rarely cover everything; scholarships and grants close the rest. See the scholarship finder.

Returns are not guaranteed and investments can lose value. This tool is for educational planning only and is not financial advice.
