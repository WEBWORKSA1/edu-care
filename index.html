Frequently asked questions

Is Edu.Care really free?

Yes. Guides, tools, the scholarship finder and the learning-plan service are free. We are supported by advertising, donations and partnerships.

Who writes the content?

The Edu.Care Editorial Team researches and writes every guide using reputable public sources, and reviews content regularly. See our editorial policy.

Will you sell my information?

No. We only share your request with a vetted partner if you explicitly opt in on the form. See the privacy policy.

Is Edu.Care connected to any school or other “Educare” organization?

No. Edu.Care is independent. See our trademark and copyright disclosure.
