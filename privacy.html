Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how Edu.Care (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use edu.care and related pages (the “Site”).

Information we collect

  • Information you provide through forms: for example your name, email, phone number (optional), country, learner level, goals, and messages. Forms include learning-plan requests, contact, newsletter, donation pledges, contest entries, job applications and partnership inquiries.
  • Automatically collected information such as browser type, device, pages viewed, and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar technologies by us and our advertising and analytics partners.
  • Tool inputs (calculators, timer) are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.

How we use information

  • To respond to requests and deliver learning plans, newsletters and contest results.
  • To share your learning-plan request with up to three vetted education partners only if you tick the optional consent box.
  • To operate, secure and improve the Site.
  • To show advertising that keeps the Site free.

We do not sell personal information.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered using a third-party form service (FormSubmit) that transmits your submission to our inbox. Please do not submit sensitive information such as government ID numbers, financial account details or health records.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube may collect data under Google’s privacy policy. News links take you to third-party websites with their own policies.

Cookies

See our cookie policy for details and choices.

Children’s privacy

The Site provides information for families and students but is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Requests concerning learners under 16 must be submitted by a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected, and for legal or record-keeping requirements, after which they are deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA or India’s DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, withdraw consent, object to processing and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. To exercise these rights, use our contact form and choose “Privacy request”.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. We rely on appropriate safeguards where required.

Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use our contact form.
