Editorial policy

How we create content you can trust.

Original, practical content

Every guide is written by the Edu.Care Editorial Team to answer real questions from students and families. We do not copy content from other websites, and we do not publish thin or auto-generated pages.

Research and sources

We rely on official sources (governments, education departments, accreditation bodies, scholarship providers), peer-reviewed research and established professional organizations. Where rules change often, such as financial aid, visas or loan terms, we point readers to the official source to confirm current details.

Review and updates

Guides are reviewed at least annually and whenever relevant rules change. Each guide shows when it was last reviewed.

Corrections

If you spot an error, tell us through the contact form (topic: “Correction or update”). We correct factual errors promptly.

Advertising and sponsorship

Advertising is served by third parties such as Google AdSense and is clearly separate from editorial content. Sponsored placements are labeled. Sponsors and partners do not review or approve our editorial content before publication.

Not professional advice

Our content is general information. It is not legal, financial, medical or psychological advice. Consult a qualified professional for decisions about your situation.

Automated content

Our news and video sections are automatically compiled from public feeds of third-party publishers and channels. Headlines link to the original source, and videos are embedded from YouTube; ownership remains with the original creators.
