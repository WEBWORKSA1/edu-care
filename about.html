About Edu.Care

Education decisions are some of the most important a family makes, and good guidance is often expensive or hard to find. Edu.Care exists to change that.

Our mission

To give every learner and every family clear, practical, trustworthy guidance — from first lessons to final exams, from choosing a preschool to paying for college — free of charge.

What we offer

  • Guides that turn research and best practice into steps you can act on today.
  • Free tools such as GPA, final-grade, student-loan and savings calculators and a focus timer.
  • A scholarship finder pointing to official sources.
  • Auto-updated news and videos from respected education sources.
  • Free learning plans, with optional introductions to vetted providers.

Our values

  • Learner first. If something is not in the learner’s interest, we do not publish or promote it.
  • Clarity. Plain language, specific steps, no jargon for its own sake.
  • Honesty. We label sponsored content, link to official sources and correct mistakes quickly.
  • Privacy. We never sell personal information.

How we are funded

Edu.Care is supported by advertising (including Google AdSense), reader donations, sponsorships and partnerships with education providers. Funding never determines our editorial conclusions. Read our editorial policy.

Independence

Edu.Care is an independent website. It is not affiliated with any government, school, or organization using the name “Educare” or similar names. See our trademark and copyright disclosure.
